# Dietary Guidance Before and After POCS Surgery

## Key Takeaway
Recovering from POCS (Per-Oral Cholangioscopy) surgery requires a gradual dietary adjustment to aid healing and prevent the recurrence of stones. The process moves from clear liquids to soft foods, eventually transitioning to a high-fiber, low-fat balanced diet. Proper hydration and avoiding irritants like alcohol and greasy foods are essential for long-term success.

***

Proper dietary guidance is crucial for patients recovering from gallstone treatment via POCS (Per-Oral Cholangioscopy). Post-operative dietary adjustments not only help with wound healing but also reduce the burden on the digestive system and prevent the recurrence of stones. This guide provides a comprehensive roadmap to help you recover quickly.

## Phase 1 (Days 1-3): Clear Liquid Diet

The first 1-3 days after POCS surgery are critical for recovery. During this stage, adhere to the following:

*   **Focus on warm liquids:** such as rice soup, vegetable broth, and clear chicken broth.
*   **Small, frequent meals:** Control intake to about 100-150ml per serving.
*   **Avoid irritants:** Do not consume cold drinks or spicy/irritating foods.
*   **Stay hydrated:** Ensure a daily water intake of at least 1500ml.

## Phase 2 (Days 4-7): Transition to Soft Foods

As digestive function gradually returns, you can transition to a soft food diet:

*   **Add easy-to-digest foods:** such as soft noodles, porridge (congee), and steamed eggs.
*   **Include steamed vegetables:** such as carrots, pumpkin, and broccoli.
*   **Introduce lean protein:** small amounts of tofu, fish, or lean meat.
*   **Continue restrictions:** Avoid fried, spicy, and high-fat foods.

> **Dietary Tip:** Keep food temperature moderate—not too cold or too hot. Eat slowly and chew thoroughly to reduce the burden on your digestive system.

## Phase 3 (Weeks 1-4): Establishing a Balanced Diet

During weeks 1-4 post-surgery, patients can gradually establish regular eating habits:

*   **Increase fiber intake:** Incorporate whole grains, fresh vegetables, and fruits.
*   **Control fat intake:** Choose vegetable oils over animal fats.
*   **Prioritize quality protein:** such as fish, poultry, and soy products.
*   **Regular routine:** Maintain small, frequent meals at set times and quantities.

## Long-Term Dietary Guidance

To prevent the recurrence of gallstones after POCS surgery, maintain scientific eating habits long-term:

*   **Control calories:** Maintain an ideal body weight.
*   **Limit high-cholesterol foods.**
*   **Increase soluble fiber intake.**
*   **Eat regularly:** Avoid binge eating or skipping meals.
*   **Hydrate well:** Maintain a daily water intake of over 2000ml.

## Foods to Avoid

Certain foods may increase bile secretion or stimulate gallbladder contractions and should be avoided or limited:

*   **High-fat foods:** Fried foods, fatty meats, cream, and animal organs.
*   **Irritating foods:** Chili peppers, curry, alcohol, and strong coffee.
*   **High-cholesterol foods:** Egg yolks (limit to no more than 3 per week), animal brains, etc.
*   **Processed foods:** Items containing preservatives and additives.

## Recommended Nutritional Support

Certain nutrients are particularly beneficial for recovery after POCS:

*   **Vitamin C:** Promotes wound healing and boosts immunity.
*   **Vitamin D & Calcium:** Maintain bone health.
*   **Probiotics:** Regulate gut flora and improve digestion.
*   **Magnesium:** Helps prevent the formation of gallstones.

## Summary

Dietary adjustment after POCS surgery is a gradual process requiring patient cooperation. Through scientific dietary management, you can significantly accelerate recovery, reduce complications, and prevent stone recurrence. If you experience any discomfort or have diet-related questions, consult your doctor or a nutritionist promptly for personalized advice.
