## Key Takeaway

Recovering from gallbladder-preserving surgery relies on three pillars: a strict **low-fat diet** to allow the gallbladder to heal, taking prescribed medication (like **Ursodeoxycholic acid**) to prevent stone recurrence, and adhering to a **regular follow-up schedule**. Early mobilization and proper wound care are also essential for a smooth recovery.

## Early Post-Operative Care (Weeks 1-2)

The first two weeks are critical for healing.
*   **Diet Progression:** You will need to fast for the first 6 hours. Once your intestinal function returns (indicated by passing gas), you can gradually transition from clear fluids to semi-liquid foods, and eventually to soft foods.
*   **Warning Signs:** Monitor your abdomen closely. If you experience persistent severe abdominal pain, fever, or jaundice (yellowing of the skin/eyes), seek medical attention immediately.

## Dietary Management Strategies

Your diet is the most important factor in preventing recurrence.
*   **The Golden Rule:** Follow a **"Low-Fat, High-Protein, High-Fiber"** diet.
*   **Strict Control (1-3 Months):** strictly limit fat intake for the first 1-3 months. Avoid fried foods, animal organs, cream, and other high-fat items.
*   **Protein Sources:** Choose lean meats, fish, and soy products.
*   **Eating Habits:** Eat regular meals and avoid overeating. **Breakfast is crucial** as it stimulates the gallbladder to contract and empty regularly.

## Wound Care & Activity Management

*   **Incision Care:** Laparoscopic incisions are small. Keep them dry and clean. Stitches are typically removed 7-10 days after surgery.
*   **Activity:** You can get out of bed for light activity the day after surgery. Gradually increase your activity level.
*   **Restrictions:** Avoid lifting heavy objects (over 3 kg) or strenuous exercise for 2 weeks. You may gradually return to normal exercise after 1 month.

## Medication & Follow-Up

*   **Medication:** You will typically need to take **Ursodeoxycholic acid** for 3-6 months to reduce the risk of stone recurrence.
*   **Check-ups:** Regular check-ups are scheduled at 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year post-op. These include liver function tests and abdominal ultrasounds.
*   **Long-term:** It is recommended to have a gallbladder ultrasound at least once a year to monitor for recurrence.

## Lifestyle Adjustments

Preventing stones is a long-term commitment.
*   **Weight Management:** Control your weight through gradual methods. Avoid rapid weight loss, which can increase stone risk.
*   **Healthy Habits:** Quit smoking, limit alcohol, and maintain a positive mood.
*   **Routine:** Regular sleep patterns and moderate exercise are the cornerstones of maintaining gallbladder function.
