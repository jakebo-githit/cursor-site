## 了解胆固醇性胆结石
胆固醇性胆结石是最常见的胆囊结石类型，其主要成分为胆固醇。当胆汁中胆固醇过饱和，或胆囊排空不畅时，胆固醇便会结晶析出，形成结石。作为肝胆外科医生，我常告诉患者：'结石或许无法完全通过饮食消失，但科学的饮食是防止其发作的关键。'

## 饮食核心原则：低脂、高纤、规律

### 1. 严格限制脂肪摄入
高脂饮食会刺激胆囊剧烈收缩，易诱发胆绞痛。建议每日脂肪摄入量控制在40-50克以下。
- **避免**：肥肉、油炸食品、动物内脏、奶油糕点。
- **替换**：选择瘦肉、鱼肉、豆制品，烹饪方式以蒸、煮、炖为主。

### 2. 增加膳食纤维
膳食纤维能结合胆汁酸并促进排出，降低胆汁中胆固醇的饱和度。多吃燕麦、玉米、糙米等全谷物，以及深绿色蔬菜和低糖水果。

### 3. 规律进食，特别是早餐
空腹时间过长会导致胆汁在胆囊内过度浓缩。按时吃早餐能促进胆囊收缩和排空，防止胆汁淤积。

## 需要警惕的'隐形'刺激物
除了高脂食物，辛辣调味料（如辣椒、咖喱）以及烟酒、浓咖啡也可能引起Oddi括约肌痉挛，增加胆道压力，应尽量减少摄入。

## 术后饮食注意事项
对于接受保胆取石或胆囊切除术的患者，术后早期（1-3个月）需严格低脂饮食，随后根据消化功能逐步恢复正常饮食，但仍需保持健康习惯以防复发。

## Understanding Cholesterol Gallstones
Cholesterol gallstones are the most common type of gallstones, formed primarily when bile contains too much cholesterol. As a hepatobiliary surgeon, I emphasize that while diet alone may not dissolve existing stones, it is crucial for preventing symptoms and complications.

## Core Dietary Principles

### 1. Limit Fat Intake
High-fat foods trigger the gallbladder to contract forcefully, which can lead to biliary colic. Aim for a low-fat diet, restricting visible fats like butter, fried foods, and fatty cuts of meat. Instead, opt for lean proteins like chicken, fish, and plant-based options.

### 2. Increase Dietary Fiber
Fiber helps reduce the absorption of cholesterol and improves bile acid metabolism. Incorporate whole grains (oats, brown rice), fruits, and vegetables into your daily meals.

### 3. Maintain Regular Meal Times
Skipping meals, especially breakfast, leads to bile stasis, where bile sits in the gallbladder and becomes concentrated. Regular meals ensure the gallbladder empties properly.

## Foods to Avoid
- **Fried and Greasy Foods:** French fries, fried chicken, and fast food.
- **Refined Carbs and Sugars:** White bread and sugary snacks can increase cholesterol levels in bile.
- **Spicy Irritants:** In some patients, spicy foods can trigger discomfort.

## Post-Surgery Considerations
If surgery is performed, a strict low-fat diet is recommended for the first month to allow the body to adapt, especially if the gallbladder is removed. Gradually reintroduce fats while monitoring tolerance.
