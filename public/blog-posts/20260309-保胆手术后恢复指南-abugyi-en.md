How soon can you return to work after gallbladder preservation surgery? This is one of the most common questions patients ask.

Gallbladder preservation surgery is a minimally invasive procedure that maintains the gallbladder's function while treating gallstones and cholecystitis. Unlike traditional cholecystectomy, this approach preserves the gallbladder's physiological functions, resulting in less impact on patients' quality of life.

## Postoperative Recovery Timeline

1. **Days 1-3 post-surgery**: Hospital observation period focusing on wound healing and pain management
2. **Weeks 1-2**: Light activity phase, allowing for simple daily activities
3. **Weeks 2-4**: Recovery phase with gradually increasing activity levels
4. **Weeks 4-6**: Basic recovery phase, most patients can return to work

## Practical Takeaways

* **Gradual activity progression**: Avoid strenuous exercise initially and gradually increase activity levels as tolerated
* **Dietary adjustments**: For the first 1-2 weeks, avoid fatty foods and follow medical advice for gradually returning to a normal diet
* **Wound care**: Keep the incision clean and dry, change dressings as recommended
* **Regular follow-up**: Attend scheduled follow-up appointments to assess recovery progress
* **Work accommodations**: Recovery time varies based on job type - office workers typically return sooner than those in physically demanding roles

## When to see a doctor

* Experiencing persistent or worsening abdominal pain, fever, or jaundice
* Noticeable redness, swelling, discharge, or unusual odor around the incision
* Persistent nausea and vomiting lasting more than 24 hours
* Unresolved shoulder or back pain
* Body temperature exceeding 38.5°C (101.3°F) for more than 24 hours
