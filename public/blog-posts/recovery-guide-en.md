# Post-operative Rehabilitation Guide: How to Accelerate Recovery

## Key Takeaway
While minimally invasive surgery offers the advantage of smaller incisions, it does not mean recovery management can be ignored. The quality of your post-operative recovery depends on four key pillars: appropriate activity, a proper diet, regular follow-ups, and the ability to identify potential risks.

## Staged Recovery Suggestions

### Phase 1 (Days 1-3)
*   **Rest & Movement:** Prioritize rest but avoid staying in bed all day; engage in basic, light movement.
*   **Diet:** Stick to a bland, easily digestible diet. Eat small, frequent meals.
*   **Monitoring:** Closely monitor any changes in pain levels, body temperature (fever), or nausea.

### Phase 2 (Days 4-14)
*   **Activity:** Gradually increase low-intensity activities, such as walking.
*   **Precautions:** Avoid lifting heavy objects and strenuous twisting motions.
*   **Diet:** Transition from soft foods to a relatively balanced diet. Continue to avoid high-fat or irritating foods.

### Phase 3 (Weeks 2-6)
*   **Daily Life:** Gradually return to normal work intensity based on your doctor's advice.
*   **Routine:** Establish a stable daily schedule; avoid staying up late and binge eating.
*   **Planning:** Adjust your ongoing management plan based on the results of your follow-up examinations.

## Managing Common Post-Operative Issues

*   **Mild Bloating/Bowel Changes:** Adjust your dietary structure and the pace at which you eat.
*   **Fat Intolerance:** Reduce the fat load in single meals and increase soluble dietary fiber.
*   **Persistent Fatigue:** Evaluate whether your sleep, nutrition, and activity levels are imbalanced.

**Important:** If symptoms persist or worsen, or if you experience fever, jaundice, or severe pain, seek medical attention immediately.

## Follow-up and Check-ups

It is crucial to complete check-ups at key time points as prescribed by your doctor. Keep a long-term record of the following to assist your medical team:

*   Changes in symptoms
*   Dietary tolerance levels
*   Weight and physical fitness status
*   Comparisons with previous examination results

These records help your doctor quickly assess your recovery trajectory and adjust your treatment plan.
