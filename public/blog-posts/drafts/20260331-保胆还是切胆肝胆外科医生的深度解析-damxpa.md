## 前言：患者面临的经典抉择

作为一名肝胆外科医生，我在门诊最常面对的问题就是：“医生，我的胆囊一定要切除吗？能不能把石头取出来，把胆保住？”这不仅是医学问题，更是患者对保留器官功能的渴望。

## 切除胆囊：百年金标准的地位

腹腔镜胆囊切除术（LC）目前仍是治疗有症状胆囊结石的“金标准”。对于胆囊壁严重增厚、萎缩、怀疑恶变或功能完全丧失的胆囊，切除是毋庸置疑的选择。它能彻底消除结石复发的土壤，避免二次手术。然而，切除后部分患者可能出现胆囊切除术后综合征（PCS），如腹泻、消化不良或反流性胃炎。

## 保胆取石：保留器官功能的希望

随着内镜技术的发展，保胆取石术重新回到视野。其核心理念是保留有功能的胆囊，维持胆汁酸的肠肝循环。根据2020年的一项综述指出，严格的筛选病人是保胆成功的关键（PMCID: PMC7598833）。如果胆囊收缩功能良好（收缩率>30%），且壁薄光滑，保胆不仅是可行的，且术后生活质量往往优于切胆。

## 复发风险：不得不谈的现实

选择保胆，患者必须接受复发率这一客观现实。这与患者的体质（成石性胆汁）有关。根据2021年关于保胆术后长期预后的临床研究，虽然部分患者面临结石复发，但通过术后药物干预可以降低风险（PMCID: PMC8034472）。我们不仅是在治病，更是在管理一种慢性代谢性疾病。

## 决策的关键考量

在2022年关于胆囊保留的研究中，学者们强调了个体化治疗的重要性（PMCID: PMC8472831）。我的建议是：
1. **看功能**：ECT显像或B超下脂餐试验证实收缩功能良好。
2. **看形态**：无萎缩，壁厚<3-4mm，无息肉。
3. **看意愿**：患者对保留器官有强烈意愿，且能接受复发风险并配合随访。

## 结语

保胆与切胆并非对立，而是针对不同病情的优化选择。作为外科医生，我们的目标不仅是取净结石，更是为患者保留最大的生理功能和生活质量。

## Introduction: The Classic Dilemma

As a hepatobiliary surgeon, the most frequent question I encounter in the clinic is: "Doctor, must my gallbladder be removed? Can't we just take out the stones and keep the organ?" This reflects a patient's natural desire to preserve physiological function.

## Cholecystectomy: The Gold Standard

Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (LC) remains the "gold standard" for treating symptomatic gallstones. For gallbladders that are severely thickened, atrophied, suspected of malignancy, or have lost all function, resection is the undisputed choice. It eliminates the "soil" for stone recurrence, thereby avoiding the need for secondary surgeries. However, Post-Cholecystectomy Syndrome (PCS), such as diarrhea or bloating, remains a concern for a subset of patients.

## Gallbladder Preservation: Preserving Function

With advancements in endoscopy, gallbladder-preserving surgery has regained attention. The core philosophy is to retain a functioning gallbladder to maintain the enterohepatic circulation of bile acids. As noted in a 2020 review, strict patient selection is the key to success in preservation procedures (PMCID: PMC7598833). If the gallbladder has good contractile function (ejection fraction >30%) and a smooth wall, preservation is not only feasible but often results in a superior quality of life compared to removal.

## The Reality of Recurrence

Choosing preservation requires the patient to accept the objective reality of recurrence rates. This is often linked to the patient's metabolic constitution (lithogenic bile). According to a 2021 clinical study on long-term outcomes, while some patients face stone recurrence, this risk can be mitigated through postoperative pharmacological management (PMCID: PMC8034472). We are not just treating a disease; we are managing a chronic metabolic condition.

## Key Considerations for Decision Making

Research from 2022 emphasizes the importance of individualized treatment strategies in gallstone disease (PMCID: PMC8472831). My criteria for recommending preservation include:
1.  **Function**: Confirmed contractile function via ECT or ultrasound fatty meal test.
2.  **Morphology**: No atrophy, wall thickness <3-4mm, and no polyps.
3.  **Patient Commitment**: A strong desire to retain the organ and a willingness to accept recurrence risks and comply with follow-up.

## Conclusion

Preservation and resection are not opposing forces but optimized choices for different pathological states. Our goal as surgeons is not merely to remove stones but to preserve the maximum physiological function and quality of life for our patients.
