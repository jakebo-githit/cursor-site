# 保胆手术后恢复指南

## 引言  
保胆手术（胆囊保留手术或胆囊取石术）是一种旨在保留胆囊功能的同时去除结石的微创技术[^1^]。与胆囊切除术相比，其恢复周期更短，但术后管理对预防结石复发和并发症至关重要。本文将提供详细的恢复时间表及实用建议。

---

## 术后恢复时间表  

### **1-3天：住院期**  
- **疼痛管理**：切口疼痛可通过医生处方的非阿片类药物控制[^2^]。  
- **早期活动**：术后6小时可床上翻身，24小时后在辅助下行走，预防深静脉血栓。  
- **饮食**：禁食6小时后逐步过渡至清流质（如米汤），避免脂肪摄入。  

### **1-2周：轻度活动期**  
- **活动**：避免提重物（>5kg）和弯腰动作，可进行短距离散步。  
- **饮食**：低脂、高纤维饮食（如燕麦、蒸蔬菜），每日饮水≥1.5L[^3^]。  
- **伤口护理**：保持敷料干燥，术后7天门诊拆线。  

### **2-4周：恢复期**  
- 逐步恢复轻度家务，建议每天步行30分钟促进肠蠕动。  
- 增加蛋白质摄入（如鱼肉、豆腐）辅助组织修复。  

### **4-6周：基本恢复期**  
- 可恢复低强度运动（如瑜伽、游泳），仍需避免腹压增高的动作（如仰卧起坐）。  

### **长期管理**  
- **饮食**：限制胆固醇摄入（如动物内脏），每日脂肪摄入<30%总热量[^4^]。  
- **复查**：每6个月超声检查胆囊状况，监测结石复发。  

---

## 实用建议  

### **活动**  
- 术后1周内避免驾驶（麻醉和止痛药影响反应能力）。  

### **饮食**  
- **推荐食物**：橄榄油、坚果（富含不饱和脂肪酸）；  
- **禁忌食物**：油炸食品、奶油（易诱发胆汁淤积）。  

### **伤口护理**  
- 若出现红肿、渗液，立即使用碘伏消毒并就医。  

### **复查**  
- 术后1个月、3个月、6个月随访，重点检测肝功能及胆囊收缩功能[^5^]。  

---

## 工作恢复指导  

### **办公室工作**  
- 术后1周可远程办公，2周后恢复坐班。  

### **体力劳动**  
- 需延迟至6周后，且需医生评估切口愈合情况。  

---

## 何时就医：警示信号  
- **感染迹象**：体温>38°C，切口化脓；  
- **严重腹痛**：持续右上腹绞痛伴呕吐，警惕胆道痉挛或结石残留[^6^]。

# Recovery Guide After Gallbladder-Preserving Surgery  

## Introduction  
Gallbladder-preserving surgery (e.g., cholecystolithotomy) removes gallstones while retaining gallbladder function[^1^]. Compared to cholecystectomy, it offers quicker recovery but requires stringent postoperative care to prevent recurrence. This guide provides evidence-based recommendations for optimal recovery.

## Recovery Timeline  

### **Days 1-3: Hospital Stay**  
- **Pain Control**: Non-opioid analgesics are preferred for effective pain management with fewer side effects[^2^].
- **Early Mobilization**: Begin turning in bed 6 hours post-surgery; ambulate within 24 hours to prevent deep vein thrombosis.
- **Diet**: Start with clear liquids (rice water) 6 hours after surgery; avoid fat intake initially.

### **Weeks 1-2: Light Activity Phase**
- **Activity**: Avoid lifting >5kg and bending; short walks are encouraged.
- **Diet**: Low-fat, high-fiber diet (oats, steamed vegetables); drink ≥1.5L water daily[^3^].
- **Wound Care**: Keep dressings dry; sutures removed at day 7 in outpatient clinic.

### **Weeks 2-4: Recovery Phase**
- Gradually resume light household chores; walk 30 minutes daily to promote bowel function.
- Increase protein intake (fish, tofu) to support tissue repair.

### **Weeks 4-6: Near-Full Recovery**
- Resume low-intensity exercise (yoga, swimming); avoid abdominal pressure activities like sit-ups.

### **Long-Term Management**
- **Diet**: Limit cholesterol (organ meats); keep fat intake <30% of total calories[^4^].
- **Follow-up**: Ultrasound every 6 months to monitor gallbladder status and detect recurrence.

## Practical Recommendations

### **Physical Activity**
- Avoid driving for 1 week post-surgery (anesthesia and pain medications affect reaction time).

### **Dietary Guidelines**
- **Recommended**: Olive oil, nuts (rich in unsaturated fatty acids).
- **Avoid**: Fried foods, cream (may trigger bile stasis).

### **Wound Care**
- Disinfect with iodine and seek medical attention if redness, swelling, or discharge occurs.

### **Follow-Up Schedule**
- Visits at 1, 3, and 6 months post-surgery to assess liver function and gallbladder contractility[^5^].

## Return-to-Work Guidance

### **Office Work**
- Remote work possible after 1 week; return to office after 2 weeks.

### **Physical Labor**
- Delay until 6 weeks; requires surgeon clearance based on wound healing.

## When to Seek Medical Attention
- **Infection signs**: Temperature >38°C, wound purulence.
- **Severe pain**: Persistent right upper quadrant colic with vomiting may indicate biliary spasm or retained stones[^6^].
