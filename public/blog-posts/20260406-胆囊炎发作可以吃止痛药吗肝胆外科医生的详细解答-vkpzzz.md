## 胆囊炎发作时止痛药使用的专业建议

作为肝胆外科医生，我经常遇到患者询问胆囊炎发作时是否可以自行服用止痛药。这是一个需要谨慎对待的问题。

## 为什么胆囊炎会引起剧烈疼痛

胆囊炎的疼痛主要源于两方面：一是胆囊壁的炎症反应，二是胆囊管梗阻导致的胆囊内压力升高。当胆结石嵌顿在胆囊管时，胆汁无法排出，胆囊收缩便产生典型的胆绞痛，疼痛常位于右上腹，可向右肩背部放射。

## 止痛药可以吃吗？

**答案是：可以，但需谨慎选择。**

### 可以考虑的止痛药：
- **非甾体抗炎药（NSAIDs）**：如布洛芬、双氯芬酸钠，具有抗炎镇痛作用，对胆绞痛有一定缓解效果
- **解痉药**：如山莨菪碱（654-2）、阿托品，可缓解平滑肌痉挛

### 需要避免的药物：
- **吗啡类止痛药**：可能引起Oddi括约肌痉挛，反而加重胆道压力，使疼痛加剧

## 服用止痛药的注意事项

1. **不要空腹服用NSAIDs**，以免刺激胃黏膜
2. **剂量要控制**，切勿因疼痛难忍而超量服用
3. **记录服药时间和剂量**，就医时告知医生
4. **止痛药只是临时措施**，不能替代正规治疗

## 何时必须立即就医

出现以下情况请立即前往医院急诊：
- 伴有高热（体温>38.5°C）
- 皮肤或眼白发黄（黄疸）
- 持续剧烈疼痛超过6小时不缓解
- 出现休克症状（冷汗、血压下降、意识模糊）
- 反复呕吐无法进食

## 根治性治疗建议

对于反复发作的胆囊炎，**腹腔镜胆囊切除术**是目前的标准治疗方案。手术创伤小、恢复快，可彻底解决胆囊炎反复发作的问题。

## Can You Take Painkillers During a Gallbladder Attack?

As a hepatobiliary surgeon, patients frequently ask me whether they can self-medicate with painkillers during a gallbladder attack. This question requires careful consideration.

## Why Does Cholecystitis Cause Severe Pain?

The pain of cholecystitis originates from two main sources: inflammation of the gallbladder wall and increased intraluminal pressure due to cystic duct obstruction. When gallstones become impacted in the cystic duct, bile cannot exit, and gallbladder contractions produce the classic biliary colic—typically located in the right upper quadrant, often radiating to the right shoulder or back.

## Can You Take Painkillers?

**The answer is yes, but choose carefully.**

### Acceptable Options:
- **Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)**: Ibuprofen and diclofenac have anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties that can help relieve biliary colic
- **Antispasmodics**: Such as hyoscine butylbromide, which relieve smooth muscle spasm

### Medications to Avoid:
- **Morphine and opioids**: These may cause sphincter of Oddi spasm, potentially increasing biliary pressure and worsening pain

## Important Precautions

1. Never take NSAIDs on an empty stomach to avoid gastric irritation
2. Control the dosage—do not exceed recommended amounts
3. Document timing and dosage for your doctor
4. Remember that painkillers are temporary measures, not definitive treatment

## When to Seek Emergency Care

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience:
- High fever (>38.5°C)
- Jaundice (yellowing of skin or eyes)
- Severe continuous pain lasting more than 6 hours
- Signs of shock (cold sweats, low blood pressure, confusion)
- Intractable vomiting

## Definitive Treatment

For recurrent cholecystitis, **laparoscopic cholecystectomy** is the standard treatment. It's minimally invasive with quick recovery, permanently resolving the problem.
