## Early Identification and Emergency Management of Acute Cholecystitis
Acute cholecystitis is a common gallbladder condition; understanding its early identification and emergency management is crucial. This comprehensive guide covers the essentials, including causes, symptoms, dietary recommendations, emergency procedures, and when to seek medical attention.

### Causes of Acute Cholecystitis
Acute cholecystitis typically results from gallstones blocking the cystic duct, leading to bile stasis and bacterial infection.

### Symptoms of Acute Cholecystitis
Common symptoms include upper abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, and fever.

### Diagnosis of Acute Cholecystitis
Doctors usually diagnose acute cholecystitis based on symptoms, physical examination, and imaging studies such as ultrasound.

### Dietary Recommendations
During the acute phase of acute cholecystitis, it is recommended that patients consume light and easily digestible foods, avoiding greasy, spicy, and other刺激性 foods.

### Emergency Procedures
If symptoms of acute cholecystitis are present, immediate medical attention is required. In the meantime, patients can take the following measures:
- Rest: Maintain bed rest and avoid vigorous exercise.
- Fasting: Avoid eating until symptoms are controlled.
- Warming: Appropriate warming can help alleviate pain.

### When to Seek Medical Attention
- Pain that worsens or becomes intolerable.
- Fever above 39°C.
- Jaundice symptoms.
- Persistent nausea and vomiting.
- Shock symptoms.
