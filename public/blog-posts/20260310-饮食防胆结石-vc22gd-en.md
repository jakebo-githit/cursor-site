Gallstones affect 10-15% of the global population and are a common digestive system disorder. The good news is that through科学的 dietary adjustments, we can effectively reduce the risk of gallstone formation.

Gallstones are primarily composed of cholesterol or bilirubin when there's an imbalance in the chemical composition of bile. Dietary factors play a crucial role in the development of gallstones.

## Practical Prevention Strategies

• **Control fat intake**: Reduce saturated and trans fats while choosing healthy unsaturated fat sources like olive oil, nuts, and fatty fish. Excessive fat increases cholesterol secretion, promoting stone formation.

• **Increase fiber consumption**: Whole grains, vegetables, fruits, and legumes contain both soluble and insoluble fiber that helps lower cholesterol levels and promotes bile flow.

• **Maintain regular eating patterns**: Avoid prolonged fasting, as regular meals help the gallbladder empty regularly, preventing bile stasis.

• **Adequate vitamin C intake**: Vitamin C helps convert cholesterol to bile acids, reducing cholesterol stone risk. Citrus fruits, strawberries, and bell peppers are excellent sources.

• **Manage weight gradually**: Lose weight slowly and healthily, avoiding rapid weight loss as it increases gallstone risk.

## When to See a Doctor

1. Severe abdominal pain, especially in the upper right quadrant, possibly accompanied by nausea and vomiting
2. Yellowing of the skin or whites of the eyes (jaundice)
3. Dark urine or pale stools
4. Fever with chills
5. Family history of gallstones with digestive discomfort symptoms
